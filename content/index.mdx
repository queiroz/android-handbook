Prepare for Android interviews one realistic question at a time. Each question starts with the answer you should be able to give aloud, then adds only the explanation, example, trade-offs, and follow-ups needed to understand it.

## Question groups

- [Android Fundamentals](/android/handbook/android-fundamentals) — questions covering the Android application model and core components.

## How to study

Start with core questions in the areas most relevant to the role. Use advanced questions after you can explain the fundamentals confidently. Deep dives are separate and optional.
